Bridging the Fragmentation in Higher Education

Legacy Systems Disconnect

  • • Isolated Portals: Separate vendors for exams, video lectures, student assignments, and campus management.
  • • Vulnerable Assessments: High cheating risk in remote assessments without real-time AI face, gaze, and tab monitoring.
  • • Non-Standard Grading: Manual spreadsheet exports prone to tampering and lack of audit trails.

The BridgeAI Operating System

  • • End-to-End Governance: Boss Admin, Super Admin, Trainer, and Student consoles operating on unified data models.
  • • AI Proctored Code IDE: In-browser compiler with test-case validation, gaze detection, and violation tracking.
  • • Cryptographic Integrity: SHA-256 verified certificates and Excel/Sheets compatible export with UTF-8 BOM encoding.

Tamper-Proof Credentials

SHA-256 Verifiable Digital Certificates

Cryptographically verifiable certificates with public validation endpoints and instant lookup.

Cryptographic Certificate Architecture

Upon passing proctored assessments, students earn digital credentials encoded with a unique SHA-256 cryptographic hash calculated from student ID, assessment ID, score, and completion timestamp.

Generated Signature Example:

e3b0c44298fc1c149afbf4c8996fb92427ae41e4649b934ca495991b7852b855

Public Verification Portal

Employers, university recruiters, and accrediting bodies can verify candidate achievement instantly without requiring portal credentials.

Public REST Endpoint:

GET /api/certificates/verify/{verificationHash}
