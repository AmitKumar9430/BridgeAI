Access Control & Governance

Creator-Only Edit & Delete Security

Strict ownership verification prevents unauthorized modification or deletion of meetings.

Client-Side Ownership Guard

canUserManageSession(sess) compares the authenticated user's ID, official email, and full name against the session record.

Invited Attendee: Join Meeting Only

Server-Side Gatekeeper Guard

verifySessionCreator(id, auth) protects every modification endpoint (PUT, DELETE, PATCH /status).
